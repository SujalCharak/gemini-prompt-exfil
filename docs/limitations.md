# Limitations

## What This Does Not Claim
- It does not prove a model is safe under all possible prompts.
- It does not guarantee absence of data leakage outside evaluated scenarios.
- It does not validate production infrastructure or access control systems.

## Coverage Gaps
- Scenario coverage is finite and must be expanded over time.
- Validators are only as strong as their evidence rules.
- Retrieval and tool behaviors are mocked unless explicitly integrated.

## Required Human Judgment
- Interpreting negative results and deciding remediation steps.
- Deciding acceptable risk thresholds for deployment.
